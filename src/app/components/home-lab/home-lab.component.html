🎯 Host Setup Golden Rules:
  • Always reserve at least 6 GB to 8 GB RAM exclusively for the host Windows operating system so your PC remains responsive.
  • Enable CPU Virtualization (Intel VT-x or AMD-V) in your computer's BIOS/UEFI settings before launching VMs.
  • Store VM virtual disks (.vhdx or .vmdk) on an SSD. Running multiple virtual machines on a mechanical hard drive will bottleneck I/O.
  • Use Internal or Private virtual switches to network the VMs together without exposing lab test traffic to your home Wi-Fi.
